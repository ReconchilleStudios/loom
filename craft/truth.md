# Truth

Product films are shown in rooms where one unbackable sentence costs more than any soft cut. Loom keeps what a film claims as carefully as how it looks.

## The truth mode

Chosen by the person at `stages/4-design.md` and written into `loom.json` and the bible.

| Mode | What the product may show | What it may never do |
|---|---|---|
| **shipped** | Only what a person can use today: real captures, or a kit that rebuilds shipped screens faithfully | Show a screen the product does not have |
| **vision, marked** | Shipped screens plus unbuilt ones, framed honestly: the product's own "Coming soon" badge, a marker on screen, an honest end card ("Reads, scores and answers today. Upload and payout ship next.") | Let a viewer leave believing a vision screen is live |
| **vision as product** | Vision screens shown as the product with no labels. For a sales demo whose owner chooses it (the Affilimates demos ran this way, at the owner's call) | Claim results, customers or numbers about the product |

In every mode the product's **capabilities** in words still come from `claims.md`. The mode changes which **pictures** are allowed, not which facts.

## The claims register

`docs/claims.md`, written in research. One row per assertion a film may make, in words or in a picture: **claim · source (file and line, URL, document) · status (shipped / vision) · safe wording**.

- **Nothing about the product's results is invented:** no "saves 10 hours a week", no "trusted by 1,000 brands", no "3x faster", no time promise, unless a source the person can show says so.
- **Numbers inside the story world are fiction and are fine:** a fictional creator's 612 orders, a fictional brand's ROAS. Numbers about the product (its users, its accuracy, its savings) are claims.
- **Banned on screen:** "AI-powered", "seamless", "real-time", "revolutionary", "the future of …", and any phrase the market research found everyone using.

## Fiction

- Every brand, customer and person in a film is fictional unless the person supplies a real one with permission.
- Fictional names are web-checked first: no real company or person with that name in the market.
- Generated people are characters, never testimonials. A stock person is atmosphere, never presented as a customer and never given a character's name.
- Real platforms may appear as plain text (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts). Their logos may not. No real brand's logo appears anywhere.

## The person's unreleased work

A film may show what the product will do. The person decides how much, through the mode. Anything they mark confidential (a client's name, an internal figure, a screen under NDA) stays out of every film and every file that leaves the machine.
